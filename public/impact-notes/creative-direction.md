# Creative Direction
**Status:** Safe

Creative directors decide what a work should convey and what reaches an audience. When a team uses generated music, that decision includes tracing the material's origin and checking the rights for its intended use.

## Latest

- **2026-07-31**: The Munich Regional Court ruled that Suno infringed copyright in songs represented by GEMA. For a director approving a soundtrack, the case illustrates why reviewing the music and checking its licensing belong in the same release decision. [Deutsche Welle](https://www.dw.com/en/german-court-rules-that-ai-music-firm-suno-violated-copyrights/a-78152227)
